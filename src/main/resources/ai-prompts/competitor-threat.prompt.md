## Criterion: competitiveThreatScore
Evaluate the direct competitive threat posed by the Target to FPT.
Preconditions:
- Requires direct FPT-specific evidence (e.g. head-to-head bid, customer loss to target, pricing pressure against FPT).
- Generic target strength or news is insufficient.
- Must never be derived from other criterion scores.
- If missing direct FPT-specific threat evidence, return suggestedRawScore=null.
